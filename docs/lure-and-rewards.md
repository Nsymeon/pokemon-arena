# Lures, hordes and participation rewards

Repel and Max Repel are now displayed as Lure and Max Lure. Their existing internal IDs (`repel`, `maxrepel`, `repel_steps`) are deliberately retained so old saves and map pickups keep working. Duration is 100/250 walked tiles. Each successful grass search while active has a 20% horde roll; water, caves and Safari do not roll hordes. Encounters still require the interact key.

A horde contains five independently rolled members of the local encounter table, all fighting simultaneously against the player's one active partner. Aim near an opponent to select it. Each has independent cooldowns/status/HP and a minimap marker. Projectiles collide with any opponent in their path; beams and area moves can hit multiple foes. Wild members do not use trainer substitutions. Switching the player's active partner remains available. Capture is allowed when only one wild Pokemon remains; running remains available throughout.

KO and capture XP use the existing game balance formula, 25 + 5 * wild level. Healthy participants split that XP; healthy bench holders of Exp. Share receive 50% each. Ordinary bench members receive no XP. Each participating healthy Pokemon receives the defeated species' EV yield, respecting held training items and 252/stat, 510 total caps. Bench Exp. Share holders receive NO EVs, a deliberate custom rule. Captures award XP but not EVs; newly caught Pokemon cannot receive their own capture XP. Existing EVs are preserved, not retroactively erased. EV berries can reduce old allocations. Exp. Share is available from the Poke Mart and equipped through the Bag.

First capture of a species opens a Pokédex entry with image, cry, FireRed description, category, dimensions and capture/level-up report. Repeated catches do not reopen it. Town music has a separate persistent Settings slider and preview; original map music IDs preserve shared themes. Lavender is reserved for the requested Let's Go version, but `assets/audio/music/lavender-lets-go.mp3` is still missing because the examined download source refused access. It is intentionally not replaced with another version. Music files and their sources/hashes are recorded in assets/audio/music/manifest.json.

Validation: Godot smoke tests cover simultaneous movement/attacks, multi-target beam damage, per-KO reward deduplication, capture entry timing, participation/bench EV separation, and audio decoding. Scripted screenshots check the horde, capture entry and Settings layouts. This is not a full manual campaign playthrough.
